About CLIMATEX

An AI-Assisted Climate Risk & Environmental Decision Support System designed for municipal resilience, multi-hazard simulation, and emergency response planning.

Mission & Purpose

Urban centers face compounding environmental stressors — rapid cloudbursts, severe heat domes, persistent winter air inversions, and infrastructure bottlenecks. Traditional monitoring tools merely report current disasters after they occur.

CLIMATEX bridges the gap between passive sensor telemetry and proactive governance by introducing a What-If Climate Simulation Engine. Decision-makers can alter rainfall, temperature, particulate concentrations, and utility levels to evaluate potential flood zones, road choke points, hospital vulnerabilities, and citizen exposure in seconds.

Explainable AI & Simulation Logic

CLIMATEX avoids opaque black-box predictions. The system leverages transparent mathematical and threshold-driven risk equations:

  • Weighted Multi-Hazard Aggregation: Flood (30%), AQI (25%), Heat (20%), Water (15%), Power Grid (10%).
  • Compound Hazard Synergy: Non-linear risk amplification when multiple critical hazards coincide (e.g. Deluge + Power Outage).
  • Topological GIS Vulnerability: Evaluates road elevation profiles against hydraulic flood thresholds to suggest real-time alternatives.

Safety & Accuracy Disclaimers

CLIMATEX is engineered as an educational, municipal decision-support, and scenario exploration system. All simulated parameters, flood boundaries, and road disruption statuses represent modelled estimations derived from baseline datasets. In active emergency scenarios, authorities must always cross-reference outputs with verified on-ground emergency dispatches and meteorological radar.
