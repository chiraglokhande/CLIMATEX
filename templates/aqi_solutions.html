Decision-support suggestions for municipal administration, transport authorities, and pollution boards.

1. Traffic & Transit Corridor Interventions
  • Intelligent Signal Phasing: Adjust automated traffic signals at identified congestion nodes to minimize stop-and-go diesel idling.
  • Promote Public Transit Subsidies: Activate temporary discount bus passes on congested radial routes during peak smog windows.
  • Heavy Vehicle Diversion: Re-route non-destined intercity freight trucks via peripheral bypass ring roads from 06:00 to 22:00.
2. Construction & Road Dust Abatement
  • Automated Water Sprinkling: Deploy continuous misting sprayers and anti-smog guns across all active construction sites exceeding 500 sq.m.
  • Mandatory Haulage Enclosure: Enforce strict tarpaulin covering for trucks carrying sand, gravel, cement, and demolition debris.
  • Mechanical Road Sweeping: Intensive vacuum sweeping on unpaved arterial shoulders to eliminate particulate resuspension.
3. Waste & Biomass Burning Zero-Tolerance
  • Dedicated Flying Squad Patrols: 24x7 ward-level enforcement squads with thermal sensors to penalize open municipal solid waste burning.
  • Door-to-Door Green Waste Pickup: Scheduled municipal collection of dry leaves and horticulture waste to prevent garden fire disposal.
  • Real-Time Citizen Reporting: Geo-tagged mobile photo reporting to immediately dispatch fire response to landfill smoke.
4. Industrial Stack & Boiler Controls
  • Continuous Emission Telemetry (CEMS): Mandatory stack emission auditing on MIDC industrial units burning coal or heavy fuel oil.
  • Temporary Industrial Curtailment: If AQI exceeds 300 (Severe), trigger Stage-3 Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP) to scale back polluting boilers.
  • Accelerated PNG / Biomass Fuel Conversion: Subsidized transitions from dirty furnace oils to piped natural gas in manufacturing belts.
